# Widgets
{: .no_toc }

Put OnSite information on your Home Screen without opening the app.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

## Adding an OnSite widget

1. On your iPhone Home Screen, long-press an empty area until the icons jiggle.
2. Tap the **+** button in the top-left corner.
3. Search for **OnSite** in the widget gallery.
4. Choose a widget size and tap **Add Widget**.
5. Tap **Done** to exit edit mode.

The widget now appears on your Home Screen.

---

## Widget content

The OnSite widget displays upcoming work and site information at a glance — so you can see what's due for the day without opening the app.

Widget content updates automatically as you add sites, jobs, and service records in the app.

---

## Configuring the widget

1. Long-press the OnSite widget on your Home Screen.
2. Tap **Edit Widget**.
3. Adjust any available options.
4. Tap outside the widget to save.
