Capture and organise your work with photos — categorised, annotated, and stored with your sites.

## Adding photos

1. Open a site from the **Sites** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Photos** section and tap **Add Photo**.
3. Choose an option:
   - **Take Photo** — opens the camera directly.
   - **Choose From Library** — opens your photo library to select one or more photos.

Photos are immediately added to the site.

---

## Photo categories

Each photo can be tagged with one category to indicate what stage of the work it shows.

| Category | Use for |
|---|---|
| **General** | Any general documentation |
| **Before** | Site condition before work begins |
| **After** | Site condition after work is complete |
| **Progress** | Mid-job documentation |
| **Issue** | Problems or snags found on site |
| **Completion** | Final sign-off shots |

To set or change the category, open a photo and tap the category label below the image.

---

## Filtering photos by category

1. In the Photos section of a site, tap the **filter** icon (or Sort menu).
2. Under **Category**, tap a category name to show only those photos.
3. Tap **All** to clear the filter.

---

## Photo captions

1. Open a photo from a site.
2. Tap **Caption**.
3. Type a short caption and tap **Save**.

The caption appears below the photo in the detail view.

---

## Photo notes

Notes are longer text entries attached to a photo.

1. Open a photo.
2. Tap **Add Note**.
3. Type your note in the **Note** field (the prompt says **Enter your note…**).
4. Tap **Save**.

Notes appear in the **NOTES** section below the photo. Multiple notes can be added to a single photo.

---

## Photo annotations (Markup)

Mark up a photo with drawings, arrows, or highlights to call attention to specific areas.

1. Open a photo.
2. Tap **Markup** (or **Edit Markup** if annotations already exist).
3. Use the markup tools to draw on the image.
4. Tap **Save** when done, or **Cancel** to discard changes.

### Clearing annotations

1. Open a photo with existing annotations.
2. Tap **Edit Markup**.
3. Tap **Clear Annotations** and confirm with **Clear All**.

---

## Sorting photos

Control the order photos appear within a site.

1. In the Photos section, tap the **Sort** menu.
2. Choose a sort option:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Upload date (newest)** | Most recently added to OnSite first |
| **Upload date (oldest)** | Oldest additions first |
| **Capture date (newest)** | Most recently taken first (uses photo EXIF date) |
| **Capture date (oldest)** | Oldest capture date first |

---

## Photo metadata

Each photo can display technical metadata from the original image file.

1. Open a photo.
2. Scroll down and tap **Advanced** to expand the metadata section.

Available metadata fields (when present in the image):

- **Place** — location name
- **Coordinates** — GPS latitude and longitude
- **Camera** — camera model
- **Lens** — lens model
- **Focal Length**
- **Aperture**
- **Shutter Speed**
- **ISO**
- **Dimensions** — pixel width × height
- **Date Taken** — the original capture time from the camera

{: .note }
If the photo has no embedded metadata, OnSite shows **Advanced information unavailable**.

---

## Navigating between photos

When viewing a photo in detail, swipe left or right to move between photos for that site. The **X of X** indicator at the top shows your position.
