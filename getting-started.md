## Install and open the app

1. Download **OnSite** from the App Store.
2. Open the app. The onboarding walkthrough starts automatically.
3. Read through the welcome screens — tap **Next** to advance, or **Skip** to go straight to setup.

---

## Choose your storage location

During onboarding you'll be asked where to save your data.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **On Device** | All data stays on this device. No iCloud account needed. Data is not accessible on other devices. |
| **iCloud Drive** | Sites, photos, notes, and files sync across all devices signed into the same Apple ID. Requires an active iCloud account. |

Tap your preferred option. You can change this later in **Settings → Storage Location**.

{: .note }
If you choose iCloud and your device goes offline, photos and files stored in iCloud may not be accessible until you reconnect.

## Set up your company details

OnSite will ask for your company information. This appears on any reports or exports you generate.

1. Tap **Add** to attach a company logo (optional).
2. Enter your **Company Name**, **Phone Number**, **Email**, and **Website**.
3. Tap **Next** when done, or skip — you can update this any time in **Settings → Company**.

---

## Enable arrival notifications (optional)

OnSite can notify you the moment you arrive at a job site, even with the app closed.

1. On the notifications screen, tap **Allow** to grant notification permission.
2. This enables arrival alerts for all sites. You can mute individual sites later in the **Notifications** tab.

---

## Create your first site

1. On the **Sites** tab, tap the **+** button.
2. Enter a **Site Name**.
3. Tap the **Address** field to search for the site location, or tap the arrow icon to use your current location.
4. Add any contacts by tapping **Add Contacts** (optional).
5. Tap **Save**.

The site now appears in your sites list.

---

## Add a job to your site

1. Tap the site you just created to open its detail view.
2. Tap **+** to add a new job.
3. Enter a **Job Name** (or choose from the job type list — tap **Job Types** to pick one or more).
4. Set a **Service Interval** if the job repeats (for example, every 12 months).
5. Set a **Due Date** if applicable.
6. Tap **Save**.

---

## Add a photo

1. Open a site and scroll to the **Photos** section.
2. Tap **Add Photo**.
3. Choose **Take Photo** to use the camera or **Choose From Library** to import.
4. Once added, tap the photo to open it and optionally:
   - Tap **Caption** to add a caption.
   - Tap a category (**General**, **Before**, **After**, **Progress**, **Issue**, **Completion**) to tag the photo.
   - Tap **Markup** to draw annotations on the image.
   - Tap **Add Note** to attach a written note.

---

## Set a service reminder

Service reminders are driven by the **Service Interval** on each job.

1. Open a site → tap a job.
2. Set **Service Interval** to the number of months between services.
3. When a service is recorded (tap **Serviced** in the Calendar tab), the next due date is calculated automatically.

OnSite tracks upcoming and overdue services in the **Calendar** tab.
