# What's New
{: .no_toc }

The latest updates and improvements to OnSite.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

## Latest version

### iCloud Integration
Store your data in iCloud Drive and sync automatically across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID. Switch storage locations at any time in **Settings → Storage Location**.

### Widgets
Add an OnSite widget to your Home Screen to see upcoming work at a glance — without opening the app.

### Annotations
Draw directly on photos to highlight issues, mark measurements, or add clarity to your documentation. Tap **Markup** on any photo to get started.

### General Fixes
Various performance and stability improvements across the app.
