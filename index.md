## What is OnSite?

OnSite is a mobile app for tradespeople to manage job sites, track work, document progress with photos, and stay on top of service schedules — all from their phone.

Whether you run one site or dozens, OnSite keeps everything organised and ready to hand.

---

## Who is it for?

OnSite is designed for anyone who works on-site: electricians, plumbers, CCTV engineers, HVAC technicians, carpenters, landscapers, and any other tradespeople who need to manage multiple job sites and recurring services.

---

## What problems does it solve?

| Problem | OnSite solution |
|---|---|
| Forgetting which sites need servicing | Service intervals and due dates per job |
| Losing before/after photos | Photo categories, captions, and annotations |
| Scattered job notes and files | Everything attached to the right site |
| Missing invoice reminders | Automatic invoice delay tracking |
| Arriving at a site cold | Arrival notifications alert you the moment you pull up |

---

## Core workflow

```
Sites  →  Jobs  →  Photos & Files  →  Schedule
```

1. **Create a site** — give it a name, address, and contacts.
2. **Add jobs** — choose job types, set a service interval, and track due dates.
3. **Document work** — attach photos (with before/after categories and annotations) and files.
4. **Check the schedule** — see which services are due and which invoices need sending.
