# Sites
{: .no_toc }

Sites are the foundation of OnSite. Every job, photo, and file lives inside a site.

## Creating a site

1. Open the **Sites** tab.
2. Tap **+** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter a **Site Name**.
4. Tap the **Address** field and type the address to search, or tap the arrow icon to fill in your current location.
5. Optionally tap **Add Contacts** to link contacts from your address book.
6. Tap **Save**.

The site appears in your list immediately.

---

## Editing a site

1. Open the site by tapping it in the list.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top-right corner.
3. Update any fields — name, address, or contacts.
4. Tap **Done** to save changes.

---

## Pinning a site

Pin your most-visited sites so they always appear at the top of the list.

1. Long-press a site in the list, or swipe right on it.
2. Tap **Pin**.

Pinned sites are shown with a **Pinned** indicator and stay at the top regardless of sort order.

To unpin, long-press the site and tap **Unpin**.

---

## Archiving a site

Archive a site when the work is complete but you want to keep the records.

1. Swipe left on a site in the list.
2. Tap **Archive**.

Archived sites are moved out of your main list and are no longer visible in the Schedule tab.

### Viewing archived sites

1. Tap the **...** or filter menu at the top of the Sites tab.
2. Select **Archived Sites**.
3. Use the search bar to find a specific archived site.

### Restoring an archived site

1. Open **Archived Sites**.
2. Swipe left on the site.
3. Tap **Restore**.

The site returns to your active list.

---

## Deleting a site

{: .warning }
Deleting a site permanently removes all its jobs, photos, files, and notes. This cannot be undone.

1. Swipe left on a site in the list.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion in the alert.

---

## Site contacts

Link contacts from your device's address book to a site so the right people are always at your fingertips.

1. Open a site and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **Add Contacts**.
3. Search for and select a contact.
4. Tap **Done**.

Contacts appear in the site detail view. Tap a contact to call, message, or email directly.

---

## Site location and map

The address you enter for a site is used to:

- Display a map pin in the site detail view.
- Power **arrival notifications** — OnSite detects when you arrive within range.
- Power **Auto-Sort** — GPS-matching photos and contacts to the right site.

Tap the map in the site detail view to open the location in Maps.

{: .note }
For accurate arrival detection, make sure OnSite has **Precise Location** permission enabled in your device Settings.
