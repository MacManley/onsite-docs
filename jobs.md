# Jobs
{: .no_toc }

Jobs track the work being done at a site — what type of work it is, how often it needs to happen, and when it's due.

## Creating a job

1. Open a site from the **Sites** tab.
2. Tap **+** to add a new job.
3. Enter a **Job Name**, or leave it blank and let the job types fill it in.
4. Tap **Job Types** to choose one or more job types (see the full list below).
5. Set a **Service Interval** if the job recurs.
6. Set a **Due Date** if there is a fixed deadline.
7. Tap **Save**.

{: .note }
You can add more jobs to a site at any time — there is no limit.

---

## Editing a job

1. Open a site and tap the job you want to edit.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top-right corner.
3. Update any fields.
4. Tap **Done**.

## Custom job types

If none of the preset types match your work:

1. Open the **Job Types** picker.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap the **Add custom job type…** field.
3. Type your custom type name and confirm.

Custom types appear in the **Custom** section of the picker and are available for all future jobs.

## Service intervals and due dates

### Service interval

A service interval tells OnSite how often a job needs to be revisited.

1. On the job edit screen, adjust **Service Interval** using the stepper (in months).
2. Set it to **0** (shown as **None**) if the job does not recur.

When you mark a job as **Serviced** in the Calendar tab, OnSite calculates the next due date automatically based on the interval.

### Due date

A due date is a one-time fixed deadline for a job — it does not repeat.

1. Toggle **Due Date** on.
2. Pick the date from the date picker.

The job appears as a flag marker on the **calendar grid** on its due date. When the date has passed without the job being completed, the flag turns red and an **Overdue** badge is shown.

{: .note }
Due date jobs appear on the calendar grid only — they do not appear in the SERVICE list on the schedule tab. If you want a job to show in the SERVICE list and recur automatically, use a **Service Interval** instead.

### Last service

Each time you tap **Serviced** in the Calendar, OnSite records a service timestamp. You'll see **Last Service: [date]** in the job detail view.

## Job status

Each job can be set to one of two statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | The job is in progress or upcoming. |
| **Completed** | The job is finished. Set a **Completion Date** to record when. |

Change the status by tapping **Job Status** on the edit screen.

---

## Job notes

Add free-text notes to any photo associated with this site's jobs. For job-level text, use the **Caption** or **Note** fields on individual photos within the site.

{: .note }
Detailed notes are stored per photo. If you need a general job note, add a photo with a caption describing the work, or use the site's Files section to attach a document.

---

## Deleting a job

{: .warning }
Deleting a job is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Open the job detail view.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete "[Job Name]"**.
4. Confirm in the alert.
